01 / About

A path that has brought me back to myself.

For more than fifteen years, I worked in international events, bringing people together across countries, cultures and very different settings. Alongside that career, an interest in the inner world never really left me.

I’m now training in transpersonal psychotherapy at CCPE in London. This is a new chapter shaped by curiosity, personal reflection and a growing commitment to work that feels meaningful and human.

03 / An area of interest

Psychedelic integration

Powerful experiences can leave us with questions, feelings or insights that take time to understand. I’m particularly interested in how people make sense of profound or challenging experiences and bring what matters from them into everyday life.

This is an area I’m continuing to learn about as part of my wider development. I do not currently offer psychedelic-assisted therapy or present myself as a qualified psychotherapist.
